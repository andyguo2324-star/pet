Pet Diary is a pet health and diary app for iPhone. Below are the questions we get most often. If yours is not here, just email us.

Where is my data stored? Is it uploaded?

All your pet profiles, photos, records, diary entries and recorded pet sounds are stored only on this device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server — we do not operate a backend and we cannot see your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone?

Because everything lives in the app's local storage, your data is included in a full iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) and is restored together with the app. You can also export records in Settings → Export (PDF / Excel report, Pet Diary Pro feature).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone (or tap Manage Subscription) and cancel from there. Cancelling only stops the next renewal — you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but the features are locked. What do I do?

Open Settings → Pet Diary Pro → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase.

What is free, and what needs Pet Diary Pro?

Free: 1 pet, all records, reminders, photo albums, the diary, the knowledge base and nearby services. Pet Diary Pro unlocks unlimited pets and PDF / Excel report export.

Why does the app ask for these permissions?

  • Photos — to add pictures to your pet's profile, albums and diary. Picked photos are copied into the app.
  • Location — to find nearby pet hospitals and shops, and to attach a place to a diary entry. Optional; everything else works without it.
  • Microphone — only to record your pet's voice so you can play it back from the cover. Optional.
  • Notifications — for vaccine, deworming and other reminders you create. Optional.

You can change any of these at any time in iOS Settings → Pet Diary.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Erase All Data removes all pets, photos, records and diary entries from the device. This cannot be undone — export a report first if you want a copy. Deleting the app also removes all local data.

Is the health information medical advice?

No. The built-in articles and care suggestions are general pet-care information and cannot replace a veterinarian's diagnosis. If your pet seems unwell, please contact a vet.

Pet Diary（宠物日记）是一款 iPhone 上的宠物健康记录与日记 App。下面是大家最常问的问题， 如果这里没有你要的答案，直接邮件联系我们就好。

我的数据存在哪里？会上传吗？

宠物档案、照片、各类记录、日记和录制的宠物叫声只保存在这台设备上， 不会上传给我们或任何服务器——我们没有后端，也看不到你的数据。详见 《隐私政策》。

怎么备份数据 / 换新 iPhone 怎么迁移？

数据保存在 App 的本地存储里，所以做整机备份（iCloud 备份或电脑备份）时会被一起备份， 换机恢复后会一起回来。也可以在 设置 → 导出 里导出 PDF / Excel 记录（Pet Diary Pro 功能）。

怎么取消订阅？

在 iPhone 上打开 设置 → Apple ID → 订阅（或点 管理订阅） 取消。取消只停止下次续订，已付的这一期仍然可以用到结束。删除 App 不会取消订阅。

已经付款但功能没解锁怎么办？

打开「设置 → Pet Diary Pro → 恢复购买」。请确认用的是购买时使用的同一个 Apple ID。

哪些免费，哪些需要 Pet Diary Pro？

免费：1 只宠物、全部记录、提醒、相册、日记、科普内容、附近服务。 Pro 解锁：不限数量宠物 + PDF / Excel 报告导出。

App 为什么要这些权限？

  • 相册——给宠物档案、照片墙和日记添加图片。选中的照片会被复制进 App。
  • 定位——查找附近的宠物医院/宠物店，以及给日记记录地点。可拒绝，其他功能不受影响。
  • 麦克风——仅用于录制宠物叫声，之后在封面播放。可拒绝。
  • 通知——你创建的疫苗、驱虫等提醒到点通知。可选。

以上权限随时可在 iOS 设置 → Pet Diary 里修改。

怎么彻底删除数据？

设置 → 清空全部数据 会删除本机的全部宠物、照片、记录与日记，且无法恢复， 需要留存请先导出报告。删除 App 同样会清空本机数据。

App 里的健康内容是医疗建议吗？

不是。内置科普内容与养护建议均为通用养护信息，不能替代兽医诊断。 如果宠物出现异常，请及时联系执业兽医。
